Contact has been a hot topic for multibody system dynamics for a long period of time. To accomplish the simulation of such problem, some special techniques are required to handle the strong nonlinear behavior caused by the discontinuity of contact. In large-scale simulation, fine-scale contact computation takes a lot of resources. To improve the computation efficiency, for some rigid contact cases in rigid-flexible coupled systems, simplified models can be put into use. To this end, in this document we mainly discuss a virtual rigid contact formulation for simplification.
